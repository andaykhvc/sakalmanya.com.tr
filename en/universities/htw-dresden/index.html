About the University

HTW Dresden is built around applied teaching, manageable study groups and close links to companies and projects. Its portfolio ranges from engineering and computer science to business, design and environmental fields. It suits students who want to move from theory into practical work early in their degree.

Typical areas of focus: Applied engineering, Economics, Design, Information technology, Environmental and agricultural sciences.

About the City

Dresden combines a strong cultural scene with research, microelectronics and reliable public transport. It is large enough to offer professional networks while remaining more manageable in daily life than Berlin or Munich.
