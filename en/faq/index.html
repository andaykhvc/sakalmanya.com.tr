FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about German, university applications, Ausbildung, documents, visas and the current pilot phase.

Do you guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Universities, authorities and other official bodies make those decisions. I help you prepare the route realistically, completely and with the relevant deadlines in view.

What German level do I need?

It depends on the goal and the specific programme. Some Ausbildung routes may start at B1, while German-taught academic programmes often require B2 or C1. We define the target first, then build the language plan.

Do you support bachelor and master applications?

Yes. I can help with university research, interpreting deadlines, documents, CVs, motivation letters and the overall application structure.

What is the difference between a university and a Hochschule?

Universities are often more research-oriented, while Hochschulen are usually more practice-oriented. The better option depends on your field, profile and goal.

Can you help with Ausbildung research?

Yes. We can compare fields and vacancies, review requirements and turn the research into realistic language and application steps.

Can parents join the consultation?

Yes. Especially for younger candidates, it can be useful for parents to understand the logic of the process.

Are paid services currently offered?

No. ŞAK Almanya is currently in a non-commercial pilot and preparation phase. No fees are accepted and no paid service contracts are created.

Do you help with motivation letters and CVs?

Yes. We work on structure, tone, completeness and a convincing application logic. The result should sound like you, not like a copied template.

How long does the process take?

Depending on the goal, German level, documents and deadlines, the process can take several months. Early planning gives you much more room.

Can everything be done online?

Yes. Consultation and planning can be handled online. Depending on the situation, phone or in-person conversations may also be possible.

Do you help after arriving in Germany?

Yes, I can guide first steps such as registration, university organization, daily structure and orientation.

Does your guidance replace official information?

No. Official sources remain decisive. My role is to make them easier to understand, flag possible gaps and organise the next practical steps.
