About the University

Paderborn University brings computer science and engineering together with business, teacher education and cultural studies. It is a good fit for applicants who want to understand technology alongside education, society and organisations rather than in isolation.

Typical areas of focus: Computer science, Engineering, Economics, Teacher education, Cultural studies.
