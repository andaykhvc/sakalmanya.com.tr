About

I am not describing this maze from the outside.

I have lived in Dresden for about three years and built this path myself: learning German, applications, housing, visa steps, documents and the first layers of the system.

I reached B2 in 7 months and earned two different C1 certificates after 17 months in total. Today I turn that experience into clear, honest and human guidance.
