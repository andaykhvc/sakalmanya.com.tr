1. About ŞAK Almanya

ŞAK Almanya is an education and consulting brand, built on its founder's own experience in Germany, aiming to provide information and support around German private tutoring and Germany university, Hochschule, Master's, and Ausbildung application processes, as well as student visa matters. Depending on the current programme, its service areas may include:

  • German private tutoring
  • Germany university applications
  • Master's degree consulting in Germany
  • Ausbildung research and consulting
  • German student visa preparation
  • Studienkolleg guidance
  • Support settling into Germany

The scope of services may change over time.

ŞAK Almanya is currently in a non-commercial preparation and pilot phase. No paid consulting, tutoring, or other paid service is currently offered through this website. A limited number of people receive free, trial-purpose guidance only; no fee is charged, and no invoice or paid service agreement is created. Paid services will begin only once the required approval and registration steps in Germany are completed.
