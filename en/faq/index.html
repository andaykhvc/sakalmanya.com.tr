Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about German, university applications, Ausbildung, visa documents and consultation.

Do you guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Admission, visa and residence decisions are made only by universities, institutions, consulates or official authorities. I help you prepare the process in a structured and realistic way.

What German level do I need?

It depends on the goal. Some Ausbildung paths may mention B1, while German-taught academic programs often require B2 or C1. We clarify the target first, then build the language plan.

Do you support bachelor and master applications?

Yes. I can help with university research, deadlines, documents, CV, motivation letters and the overall application structure.

What is the difference between a university and a Hochschule?

Universities are often more research-oriented, while Hochschulen are usually more practice-oriented. The better option depends on your field, profile and goal.

Can you help with Ausbildung research?

Yes. We can review fields, requirements, deadlines, cover letters, CVs and realistic language targets together.

Can parents join the consultation?

Yes. Especially for younger candidates, it can be useful for parents to understand the logic of the process.

Is the first consultation free?

This may vary depending on timing and scope. Message me briefly on WhatsApp and we can clarify the best format transparently.

Do you help with motivation letters and CVs?

Yes. I support structure, tone, completeness and a clean application logic. The text should fit the person, not sound like a template.

How long does the process take?

Depending on the goal, German level, documents and deadlines, the process can take several months. Early planning gives you much more room.

Can everything be done online?

Yes. Consultation and planning can be handled online. Depending on the situation, phone or in-person conversations may also be possible.

Do you help after arriving in Germany?

Yes, I can guide first steps such as registration, university organization, daily structure and orientation.

Does your guidance replace official information?

No. Official sources are always decisive. My role is to make the information understandable and structure the path practically.
